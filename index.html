A little
about me.

I’m a PhD student in the Lampe Joint Department of Biomedical Engineering at UNC–Chapel Hill and NC State. My research uses MRI-based analysis and computational modeling to study musculoskeletal biomechanics.

I develop reproducible workflows for image-based measurements and use those measurements to inform biomechanical models.

02

Research
interests.

  • Medical imaging

    MRI-based analysis and reproducible measurements of biological tissues.

  • Musculoskeletal biomechanics

    Relationships between tissue structure, mechanical function, and movement.

  • Computational modeling

    Integrating image-derived measurements into biomechanical models.

I’m interested in collaborations involving movement, injury, rehabilitation, or model validation.
